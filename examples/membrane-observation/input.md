## 原段

The fixture has a base, a cap and a membrane. The cap and base are annular. The membrane is circular and has an active layer above a support layer. The two layers remain together. The cap and the base have aligned holes. The edge is held when the cap is on. The membrane can be seen in the hole. The cap is removable. Removing it exposes more of the membrane. The membrane stays on the base. No performance tests are supplied.

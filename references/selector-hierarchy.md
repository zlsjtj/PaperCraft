# 同一测量池的入口选择示例

这是原创教学材料，无性能测量，也不声称旋塞原理具有创新性。图源见配套 FigureCraft 的 `examples/rotary-selector/`；本文段落独立可读，不要求安装另一技能。

原始事实：一个测量池，两路储液槽 A/B；传统 L 形旋塞在两个相差 90° 的角位置分别接通 A 或 B，同时关闭另一路。两图表示同一装置的替代状态。停流后才切换。候选设计保留测量池，改变入口选择；没有测量切换延迟、残留、泄漏或体积节省，也未与双测量池方案比较。

入口问题示例：

> A conventional L-port valve can select two reservoirs. Two chambers are another possible arrangement, but this has not been compared. No reduction in volume, leakage or switching time has been measured. Our arrangement shares a measurement chamber and rotates the valve by 90 degrees. Switching requires stopped flow.

该段事实没有错，但替代方案和未测指标先于设计作用，读者要到末尾才知道装置改变什么。

修订入口：

> The arrangement switches the reservoir feeding a single measurement chamber while retaining that chamber. A conventional L-port valve connects A in one position and B after a 90-degree rotation, closing the other inlet in each state. Switching takes place with flow stopped. The drawing establishes the two specified connections; it does not demonstrate a performance improvement.

方法承接：说明两种连通关系和停流切换。讨论承接：

> A separate chamber for each reservoir is an unevaluated alternative. Switching delay, carryover, leakage and occupied volume have not been measured, so the arrangement cannot yet be ranked against that alternative.

得到：先辨认设计对象和变化，继承的旋塞原理嵌入机制句；直接影响操作含义的停流条件仍留入口。失去：入口不再逐项列出未测指标，须到讨论查具体缺口。未测指标有实际承接段，而非仅在编辑备注里声称后移。无测量时不强造主结果、不把连接示意当实验。
